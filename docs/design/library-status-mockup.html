Library status for Kodi and AirPlay

Replaces the "Check library for conversion" text box with a page you can open any time. Every movie and episode in Google Drive › Kodi Downloads, grouped by where it is: ready to play anywhere, being prepared, downloading, waiting, or kept as it is. Try the filter and tap a row.

The page · tap a row for its details
A row's details (waiting for space)

What it shows

  • One number at the top: how much of the library is ready for Kodi and AirPlay (a ring like the pill's), with a colour per state underneath.
  • Phone space, in plain words: what Kodi is using for the movie it is preparing now, what is free, and the 5 GB it always leaves you. With streaming from Drive (r14), a movie needs about its own size, not twice.
  • Grouped by state, the active work first: Preparing now (poster in its ring, the step, pause), Downloading (Elementum, then Drive), Waiting (in order, with why: queued, needs X GB free, or retrying after a problem), Ready (green check, quality and size), Kept as it is (cannot be prepared without losing something).
  • Filter: All · Ready · To do · Downloading. "To do" is everything not ready yet.
  • Tap a row for its details and, where it makes sense, an action: Prepare next moves it to the front (for one waiting on space: Prepare first when there's room), Try again after a problem, Keep as it is takes it out of the queue.
  • Check now at the bottom asks Kodi to look at Drive again; the page fills in as it finds things, so you never stare at a blank box.
