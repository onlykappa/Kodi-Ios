Subtitles menu: three new directions

The current menu (player-subtitles-audio-mockups.html, board 1) is a narrow dropdown that hides Timing and More behind a scroll. All three options below show every control at once, keep one white action (Download and apply), and keep every tap 44 pt or larger. Same features as today: Off, the tracks with SDH / FORCED tags, Download and apply, Timing, Choose another, Add from a file. Nothing new is added. Tracks wrap onto more lines when there are many, so no text is ever cut off.

OPTION A

Bottom bar

A bar along the bottom, above the progress bar. The tracks are chips that wrap to extra rows when there are many; the video stays visible above it. One tap on a chip switches the subtitles.
