MOCKUP · not a Kodi render

Game rows, Apple Sports style

Only the game rows change. The page headers, the Live now card, "See all", the day headers and the Upcoming / Leagues tiles stay exactly as they are today. Each row is one line of the same height: away logo and name on the left, the time in the middle, home name and logo on the right. The logo stands on the card with nothing behind it (no circle), the way Apple Sports shows it. "No sources yet", the league badge and the chevron are gone; the middle small line is the league on Upcoming and the day on the hub.

Logos in this mockup are plain stand-in shapes. The app fetches each team's real transparent logo from ESPN at runtime.
