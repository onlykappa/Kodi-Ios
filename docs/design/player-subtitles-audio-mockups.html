Subtitles and Audio menus

Today the CC and waveform buttons open Kodi's settings dialog, a large centred box with an empty side panel. These boards replace both with one compact menu that drops from the button you tapped. On top: Download and apply, one tap that finds, downloads and turns on the best subtitles for this video in your language. Below it: the tracks, then Timing and More.

The video keeps playing. Tapping outside the menu, the ✕ or the same button closes it.

BOARD 1

Subtitles

One white button, the only primary action. The hint under it says what it will fetch (your subtitle language from Settings). Tracks are one line each; a small tag says what is special about one (SDH, FORCED). Tap a track to switch. The menu stops above the progress bar and scrolls; the fade shows there is more (Timing, More).

Measurements (pt)

  • Menu 318 wide, 64 from the top, at most 326 high (it stops above the progress bar and scrolls, with a fade), right edge under its button; radius 18; SURFACE2 at 96 % (Kodi cannot blur, §2); soft shadow. Controls behind dim to 30 %; the open button gets an 18 % white disc.
  • Header 50: title 17 bold, ✕ in a 44 × 44 area (28 disc). Sub-page header: blue "‹ Subtitles" back.
  • Download and apply: white capsule 44 high, black 16 bold, one per view (§4.5). Working: 12 % white with spinner. Done: green 16 % tint, green text.
  • Rows 44 high in an inset group (radius 12, black 28 %): title 16 (selected 16 semibold), tags 11 bold capitals in a hairline box, check 16 in 4FA8FF, hairline separators.
  • Steppers: − and + each in a 40 × 44 area; value 15 tabular. Timing steps 0.1 s (subtitles) / 25 ms (audio); hold repeats.

How Download and apply would work (for review, not decided)

  • Uses the subtitle add-on Kodi already runs for "Download subtitle…" (its search then download actions), in the language from Kodi's subtitle settings, matched by the video's file name and hash first, then by title, year and episode.
  • The best result is downloaded and turned on with xbmc.Player().setSubtitles(); it is remembered for that video so the next play has it.
  • Choose another lists the same search results. Which subtitle add-on is installed, and whether it needs an account, must be checked before building.
  • Tracks come from Player.GetProperties (subtitles, audiostreams); switching uses Player.SetSubtitle / Player.SetAudioStream; Timing and Volume boost call Kodi's subtitle delay, audio delay and volume amplification.

Not shown

  • Portrait (video is landscape only). No live blur in Kodi. VoiceOver and Dynamic Type are not available in Kodi-drawn UI (§5.3).
