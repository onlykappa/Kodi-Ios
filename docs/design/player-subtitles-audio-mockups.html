Subtitles and Audio menus

Today the CC and waveform buttons open Kodi's settings dialogs (Enable subtitles, Subtitle offset, Browse for subtitle…), a centred box with an empty side panel. These boards replace both with one compact menu that drops from the button you tapped, like the system player: the tracks you can pick first, one tap to switch, then timing and the less-used actions.

The video keeps playing and stays visible on the left. Tapping outside the menu, the ✕ or the same button again closes it.

BOARD 1

Subtitles: pick a track

"Off" is the first choice, so there is no separate "Enable subtitles" switch. Each track shows its language and what it is (SDH, Forced, From the file / Downloaded). The check marks the one in use. Offset and the extras sit below a gap, under the fold if the list is long (the list scrolls).

BOARD 4

After a pick

Picking a track switches at once and closes the menu after 0.3 s; a short confirmation fades in at the top for 2 s. Timing and Volume boost keep the menu open so you can tap again.

Measurements (pt; Fuse's landscape canvas converts them in the build)

  • Menu: 330 wide, top 58 (under the buttons), right edge under the button that opened it; corner radius 14; SURFACE2 at 96 % (Kodi cannot blur, §2); soft shadow.
  • Header 48 high: title 17 bold; ✕ in a 44 × 44 touch area with a 28 disc.
  • Rows at least 44 high; title 16, detail 12 in GREY; check 16 in blue text 4FA8FF; separators SEP inset 44. A 8-high gap splits tracks from settings.
  • Steppers: − and + each in 44 × 44 touch areas; value 15, tabular figures.
  • The list scrolls inside a maximum height of 290 with a fade at the bottom when more is below.
  • The rest of the controls dim to 35 % while a menu is open; the button that opened it gets an 18 % white disc.

Kodi build notes (for review, not decided)

  • Replaces what the CC and waveform buttons open (osdsubtitlesettings, osdaudiosettings) with a custom dialog over VideoFullScreen; its list is filled by script from Player.GetProperties (subtitles, audiostreams) and switches with Player.SetSubtitle / Player.SetAudioStream.
  • Timing and Volume boost call Kodi's own actions (subtitle delay, audio delay, volume amplification); "Find subtitles online" opens Kodi's subtitle search; "Add from a file" opens its file browser.
  • Same tokens and helpers as the video controls (player_osd.py); no new colours, radius or button variant except the stepper.

Not shown

  • Portrait (video is landscape-only). No live blur (Kodi). VoiceOver and Dynamic Type are not available in Kodi-drawn UI (§5.3).
