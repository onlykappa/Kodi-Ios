Player controls: Apple-style scrubber

Our existing Video Controls restyled to match Apple's own player bar: a thin rounded scrubber, elapsed time on the left, remaining time with a minus on the right. It is Kodi skin XML, not Apple's control, so it keeps working with Kodi's player (resume, subtitles, Continue Watching). Everything else on the screen stays as approved.
