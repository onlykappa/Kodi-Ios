One Pace: episodes with no English dub

One Pace appears in search as a show; its arcs are the seasons. An episode plays in English dub when one exists. When it does not, it plays in Japanese with English subtitles (the subtitles are part of the picture in these files, so they cannot be switched off), and the app says so before it starts. Cancel is the bold default.
