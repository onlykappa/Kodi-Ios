What this is. The page behind the purple Leagues tile, and what a league opens. As you said, a league is just a good way to look at only those games: tapping one opens the same Live Now cards and the same Upcoming list you approved, filtered to that league. So nothing new is drawn for the games themselves (board 4 shows it); only the list of leagues is new.

Each league has an easy label, its name, and the one thing worth knowing: how many games are live and when the next one is. The sports are in sections. Evidence class: mockup, not Kodi.

To decide

  • Which list? A, by sport (I recommend it: it keeps working with many leagues), or B, tiles like the add-on’s row of logos.
  • Order. Sports with something live first, then the rest in a fixed order; inside a sport the leagues with something live first. OK?
