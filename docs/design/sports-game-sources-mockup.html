What each part says. The picture chips (1080p · 30 fps · 4.8 Mbps) are measured: either by “Check quality” (for our own sites: the app opens the stream's playlist and reads its size and bitrate, without playing it) or from what Kodi reported the last time you played that source. A source whose size is not known shows no chips. The dot is the track record on this phone: green worked recently, orange shaky, red failed lately, grey never tried. “Best” is given to one source only, and only from evidence (a source that worked, with the sharpest picture and the steadiest record); until something has been played nothing is called best. Play uses the same order. Crew's and The Loop's links cannot be tested ahead; they get their chips after their first play.

Game info keeps what is shown today (league, start, venue, records when ESPN has them). It moves under the sources and becomes a card of facts instead of one tappable row. Nothing here is added that the app cannot know.

Text rules. Names and lines wrap, never cut off with “…”. Row height grows with the text. Colours follow the Kodi iOS tokens (grey 60 %, blue text 4FA8FF, green, orange, red as status only).
