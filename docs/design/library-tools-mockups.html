Mockup · four pages · not a Kodi render

Phone space, Queue, History and AirPlay check

Each phone shows one page, with where you reach it above it. The figures are from your logs and screenshots (48 GB free, Fall 2, Inception AV1, Interstellar, the TCL Roku TV). Buttons work: try Clear, the arrows in Queue and the day switch in History.

Library status › tap “Phone space”See what Kodi keeps on the phone and clear what is safe.
Library status › Waiting folder › “Edit order”The whole line in one place; move a video up or down.
Transfers › “History” (top right)What finished each day, how long it took and how fast, and anything that failed.
Play on TV sheet › “Last time” · or tap an AirPlay notificationWhat happened on the last Play on TV, in plain words.

What each page does

  • Phone space. One bar split by what uses the space: artwork cache, Elementum leftovers, temporary files, add-on data and Kodi itself. Each line says if it is safe to clear; Clear removes it and the bar shrinks. Elementum leftovers are files from torrents that are already on Google Drive.
  • Queue. Converting now stays on top. Everything waiting is listed in order with ▲ ▼ (Kodi has no drag and drop on iPhone, so arrows, 44 pt each). Downloads to Drive have their own line. "Keep as it is" is still in each video's sheet.
  • History. Today, Yesterday and the last 7 days. Each row: what, size, how long, average speed, and the result (ready, on Drive, failed and why). The top shows the week's total and the busiest day.
  • AirPlay check. The last Play on TV as steps: the TV it went to, how the picture was sent (as it is, or converted to H.264 because the TV needs it), whether the TV asked for the video, and how it ended. A failure says what to try.
