What is real today, and what needs building (for the owner to decide)

  • Brightness, Contrast, Sharpness: Kodi 21 already has these in its video settings (per file, or as the default); the menu sets them through Kodi. Feasible in app code.
  • Saturation: to be checked on the iPhone build: if Kodi 21's renderer there has no saturation control, it needs a small change to Kodi's video shader in our Kodi build (the portrait patch), then a native build.
  • AI Upscale: Kodi has no AI upscaler. Real AI upscaling would be new native work in our Kodi build: a Metal / Core ML super-resolution step on each frame (for example Apple's MetalFX upscaler). It is the biggest item here, uses battery and heat, and has to be measured on the iPhone first. A cheaper first step is "Enhance" = Kodi's best built-in scaler (Lanczos3) plus sharpening, honestly named.
  • 1440p and 2K: on phones and TVs "2K" usually means 1440p, while cinema 2K is 2048 × 1080 (about 1080p). Shown here as the owner listed them; worth deciding whether to keep both steps or show one.
  • Per show: our own feature: the app remembers settings per TMDb show or movie and applies them when playback starts.
  • AirPlay: the TV draws the picture; phone-side changes do not reach it (board E). Converting with the picture baked in would mean a full re-encode on Drive, not proposed.
