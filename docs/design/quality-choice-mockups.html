When the quality you chose does not exist

Owner (2026-11-14, build 415): Repair kept queueing a "1080p" Naruto Shippuden pack whose season 2 is really 640×480; each copy was refused and searched again, and Notifications filled with "Repair" and "Not the quality you picked". "Notify me saying hey this isn't the quality you asked and the best one is xxxx, do you approve or would you like this one instead." These boards replace the loop with one question per season.

Reuses: the Notifications list (notifications_skin), the choice sheet (choice_sheet: heading, reason line, rows with a glyph tile and a grey detail line, Cancel), the show page card and its capsule (DEC-047), colour tokens §4.3. No new colour, font size, radius or button style.

BOARD A

One notice per season, not one per episode

The first refused copy of a season stops that season's automatic searches and posts one notice. No more "Repair: looking up … again" / "Not the quality you picked" pairs for every episode. Tapping it opens board B.

Behaviour behind the boards (already in code where marked)

  • Done (ISSUE-264): a refused copy counts for the whole season's pack, so Repair no longer picks it again as "1080p".
  • Until these boards are approved, the app takes the best real version automatically (owner, earlier: "select highest quality available").
  • After approval: the first refusal or a fallback in a season asks (board A/B) instead of downloading; the answer is kept per season (and per show with "Always").
