Sports › Live Now · round 2

What this is. The page behind the big red tile. Your screenshot is a game page: the teams, the clock and a list of what you can watch. On a phone it becomes a plain list. Every game shows an easy league label (NFL, NBA, UCL, NHL), who is playing whom, the score and how far into the game it is (quarter and clock, period, or the minute); the sources are listed either on the game’s own page (A), under each game (B), or as the rows themselves (C).

The league chips at the top filter the list (All is selected). The clock reads “Q3 · 8:41”, “P2 · 8:40” or “67′”; the longest real team names are used so you can see them wrap. Evidence class: mockup, not Kodi.

To decide

  • Which option? A (simplest, one extra tap), B (sources under each game; I recommend it) or C (one row per source, like the add-on).
  • Order of the games. Closest to the end first, or grouped by league in a fixed order (NFL, NBA, UCL, NHL)?
  • Tapping a source. Plays it at once (as drawn), or opens the usual “How would you like to watch” sheet with Play on TV?
