One activity pill for everything running in the background

That loader is Kodi's background progress box (Kodi calls it DialogExtendedProgressBar; add-ons open it with DialogProgressBG). It isn't only Elementum: Kodi itself, Elementum, Burst, Umbrella, TMDb Helper and Skin Variables all write into it. It has room for a heading, one line of text and a progress bar. When several jobs run, Kodi flips between them every 2 seconds. On the phone it is Fuse's landscape strip shrunk into whatever page is open, which is why it's tiny and turns up everywhere. These mockups replace it with one activity pill. It shows who is working (an app tile), what it's doing in plain words, and real progress. With several jobs it shows them all at once instead of flipping, and tapping it opens a sheet listing every job.

A ring around the app's tile; the bottom hairline fills too.

No percentage

Kodi jobs without a percentage get a spinner instead of a ring.

Several jobs

Stacked tiles and a count. Tap for the Activity sheet.

Paused

Elementum downloads only: grey ring, ▶ resumes.

Finished

Green check for 4 seconds, then it slides away. Downloads also leave a message in the bell.

Problem

A red tile; tap to read it in Notifications › Problems.

How it would be built

The box itself

  • Kodi's DialogExtendedProgressBar (labels 30 heading, 31 text, 32 progress) is redrawn as the pill
  • Kodi flips its jobs every 2 s; our service reads 30/31/32 each time and keeps a list of every job it has seen

Richer details

  • Elementum downloads: its engine's /torrents/list (title, %, speed, pause)
  • Drive upload: driveupload
  • Everything else: the rewrite rules above, keyed on the heading

Placement

  • Tab pages: 8 pt above the tab bar
  • Other pages: bottom edge
  • Full-screen pages: compact, top
  • Video: compact, only with controls

Tapping

  • Kodi's box can't take taps, so the pill is drawn by our pages and the Kodi box itself is hidden
  • Tap opens the Activity sheet; ❚❚ pauses Elementum
