K · FAVOURITES MANAGER

Settings › Favourites opens a page to manage favourites, not the poster grid

Today Settings › Favourites opens the Library’s poster grid. The manager is its own page: how many favourites there are, what still needs a look after an import (answers, names not found, duplicates), each imported list, and the tools (Import a List, Order, Open Favourites). The Library’s Favourites row stays the place to browse and play them. Numbers are from your import on build 486 (96 names: 58 in, 21 waiting for an answer, 20 not found).

Tapping a poster adds it to Favourites at its place in the list. These replace the I4 question sheet.
