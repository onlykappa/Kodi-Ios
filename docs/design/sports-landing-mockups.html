Sports: landing page · round 1

What this is. The first page of the sports section, before any code. Library › Sports opens a landing page with three tags: Live Now, Upcoming and Leagues. What is inside each tag comes in the next round; this round is only the Library row, the landing page and the map of pages it leads to.

Why not two rows like the add-on. The add-on’s hub (your screenshot) has a row of three banners and a row of leagues that scroll sideways on a TV. On a phone each tag becomes a full-width, thumb-sized tile or row, so everything is one tap from the top of the page.

Evidence class: mockup (browser rendering of the intended look, not Kodi). Nothing here is built yet.

States of the recommended option (A)

The same states the rest of the app has (APPLE-UI-POLICY section 6): real structure straight away, one clear empty message, one clear error with one button.

  • Which option? A, B or C for the landing page (I recommend A).
