Subtitles: hold a track to remove it or find another

Builds on the right drawer you picked (option C). Holding a track opens a small menu: Find another shows the other results for that language, and Remove from this video deletes a subtitle that was downloaded or added from a file. A track that is part of the video file cannot be deleted, so its menu only offers Find another. Holding is a shortcut: the same actions are on the Timing and more page (state 5), as the UI policy asks. Removing always asks first with Cancel.

STATE 1

Hold a downloaded subtitle

Hold English: the row lights up and a menu offers Find another English and Remove from this video (red).
