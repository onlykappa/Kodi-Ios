MOCKUP

Favourites import and the anticipated-episodes bell

Two requests, one draft PR on its own branch. G: the season Download sheet drops “Follow new episodes” and a bell on the show page takes over, with Alert and Auto-download switches. H–J: Favourites in the Library, importing a list (for example from Notes) matched through TMDb Helper, a report of duplicates and questions, sorting, and opening a favourite.

Evidence class: mockup (APPLE-UI-POLICY §9), not a Kodi render. Nothing here is built until you approve it. Titles and numbers are examples. The bell is a code-drawn outline icon, not an SF Symbol.

G · ANTICIPATED EPISODES BELL

A bell on the show page replaces “Follow new episodes”

The season Download sheet keeps only Full season and Choose episodes. A bell outline sits in the top-right corner of the show page, opposite Back, and only when the show has anticipated (not yet aired) episodes. Tapping it opens one small sheet with two switches, so a show can alert you without ever downloading.

I · IMPORT A LIST

Paste or share a list, match it with TMDb Helper, then review what needs a look

Import takes the text of a list (copied from Notes, or shared to Kodi from the Notes share sheet). Only the show and movie names are taken from each line; numbers, ticks, emoji and side notes are ignored. Every line is looked up with TMDb Helper; the list keeps its order. A report at the end says what matched, what was a duplicate and what needs your answer.

J · OPENING A FAVOURITE

Tap a favourite and it asks how to watch, or carries on where you stopped

Favourites is a fast finder. A movie you started continues from where you left off. A show opens its page, with the episode you were last watching marked so Continue is one tap; an episode you left part-way offers Continue.
