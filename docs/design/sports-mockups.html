Sports: three cleaner pages and a league filter

Owner request: remake the three Sports pages (the hub, Upcoming and Leagues) so they look cleaner, and add a detailed filter so you can pick which leagues show: NFL, NBA, golf, boxing and so on. Choose only NFL and only NFL games appear, everywhere.

How it works: one Edit button at the top of every Sports page opens the same sheet, with one switch per league grouped by sport. The choice is saved and applies to all three pages. When a filter is on, the Edit button shows how many leagues are on, and the page says so under its title. Sample teams, times and counts are made up.

BOARD A · THE THREE PAGES, CLEANER

Hub, Upcoming and Leagues

Same content as today with less empty space: the hub shows what is next instead of three big blank cards, Upcoming gets a day header per day with the league tag and the time on the left, and Leagues becomes a compact list with each league's next game. The refresh icon stays, and Edit joins it.

What is reused

  • The app's grouped rows, section headers, chips, glass-style sheet, green switches and the white capsule button (portrait.py: row, pill_button, page_header, the choice sheet). No new colour, font size or control.
  • The league tag and time column are plain text in the existing small and body fonts.

Decisions for you

  • Edit button: a text capsule at the top right beside refresh. It turns white with a number while a filter is on. OK, or would you rather have a filter icon?
  • One filter for all three pages: the same choice applies to the hub, Upcoming and Leagues. Say if Upcoming should have its own.
  • Default: every league on, as today, until you pick.
  • "Only" shortcut next to each sport turns that sport on and the others off.
