Inside the Settings pages

The Settings home is approved. Inside it, Kodi's own pages still show their desktop roots: a row of category chips, "- Theme" dashes for sub-settings, one help paragraph stuck at the bottom, an "Expert" button, and Fuse's landscape Skin Settings letterboxed in the middle of the phone. These boards bring every inside page to the same grouped-list style as the home screen.

Rules for every inside page

Structure

  • Back chevron + centred title, then a large title
  • Kodi categories = rows (no chips)
  • Related settings share a titled group
  • One footer per group explains it
  • Reset to Defaults last, in red

Controls

  • On/off: iOS switch (green)
  • Lists: value + chevron → picker page with a tick
  • Numbers: − / + stepper, 44 pt targets
  • Text: edited on the row with the keyboard
  • Unavailable: dimmed to 38 %

Words

  • Title Case labels, no "- " prefixes
  • Plain words first ("Announce to Other Devices"), Kodi's term in the footer
  • Desktop-only settings hidden on the phone (Stereoscopic 3D)
  • "Expert" button → level menu (Basic · Standard · Advanced · Expert)

Look (same as Settings home)

  • Group SURFACE 1C1C1E, radius 26 px, 16 px side margins
  • Rows ≥ 52 px, label 17 px, value GREY
  • Tiles: black, coloured glyph
  • Blue fills 0A84FF, blue text 4FA8FF in the build
  • Mockup only: Kodi draws static surfaces, no blur
