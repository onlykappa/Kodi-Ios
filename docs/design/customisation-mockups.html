Kodi for iOS · Design mockups · round 4

Customisation, without Arctic Fuse's boxes

Every page under Settings › Customisation still opens one of Fuse's landscape windows shrunk into the middle of the phone. That covers Hubs, the widget editor, the indicators/info page/video controls switch lists, the view-type picker and Skin Settings. These mockups redo each one as a real iOS page: switches instead of tick circles, drag to reorder instead of ↑ ↓ buttons, real choices instead of cycling values, and previews where it helps. Every option Fuse has is kept and writes the same skin setting, so nothing is lost.
