Sports: the game page

Owner request: when you tap a game, show a page like Apple Sports (both team crests, the time or score, one clear action), and make Follow useful: it asks to send a reminder to this phone, and when a link is available the reminder is replaced by a Play button that uses the best source.

Real team crests are fetched on the phone and never committed; this file shows neutral rings, and the preview sent to the owner has the real crests. Sample teams, times and sources are made up. Betting odds from the reference are not included.

What is reused

  • The app's white primary capsule, the grey secondary capsule, the grouped rows, the green switches, the choice-sheet look and the BEST / GOOD source chips (the Sports sources already carry a quality, a language and a best/good tag). No new colour, font size or control.
  • The team crests and league logos come from ESPN's image host at runtime and stay on the phone, never in Git (UI policy §5.4). A team without a crest shows a plain ring.

Decisions for you

  • Name the button "Follow" or "Remind me"? Your note says Follow; "Remind me" says what it does. The mockup uses Remind me.
  • Betting odds and standings from the reference are left out. Say if you want standings (a "Standings" card under Sources).
  • The reminder is a notification on this phone while the app is closed; iOS can delay it, so it works best with notifications allowed for Kodi.

After approval
