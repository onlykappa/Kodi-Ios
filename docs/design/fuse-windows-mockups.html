Mockup · round 1 · not a Kodi render, not a device screenshot

Arctic Fuse and Kodi windows still on screen

One board for each window in the roadmap list (U.1 to U.13) that is still Kodi's or Fuse's own look, plus the Volume boost row for the Audio menu (B.2). They reuse the approved pieces: the bottom choice sheet (Settings pages, board 13), the Subtitles and Audio menu (PR #17), the activity pill and sheet (round 4) and the All Links page. Skipped on purpose: U.8 and U.11 (removed by the bug fixes) and U.14 (settings, add-ons, favourites, PIN: PR #17 and #18).

Phones are drawn at iPhone 16 Pro point size (portrait 402 × 874, video 874 × 402). Artwork is placeholder gradient. Kodi cannot blur, so every translucent surface is a flat, nearly opaque texture, not Liquid Glass. Yellow tags on a screen are review notes, not part of the UI.
